# Chip

A white pill with a small orange dot, for short promises listed under a section intro.

Markup: `ul.ect-chips` with `li.ect-chip` items; the dot is drawn by CSS. The consumer supplies two to four short phrases.

- Phrases stay under six words and state facts: "Termen scris în ofertă", "Un singur om de contact".
- One column on mobile, two from 600px, centred. 15/21 weight 300; 16/22.4 from 1025px.
- Chips are not links or filters; nothing happens on hover.

Hand-written from `ect-pages/home.css` (`.ect-process__chips`).
