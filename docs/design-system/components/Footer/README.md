# Footer

The shared footer is a full-width band with the extended office-balcony illustration (`1858×941`) spanning 100% of its surface edge to edge: the original 16:9 scene occupies the right 90% (two teammates on an office balcony above a tree-lined city), while seamlessly outpainted cityscape continues across the left 10% so there is zero left-edge fade. The links sit on the open white sky at the top left, as in the Metricra reference.

`footer.ect-footer` retains its Elementor classes for the fixed back-to-top control. The `.ect-footer__top` grid contains the brand, two navigation landmarks and the contact column with the address and hours. Legal links and the coffee-cup signature follow inside `.ect-footer__inner`. The decorative `.ect-footer__art` layer is hidden from assistive technology. The DOM order is the same at every width.

- The footer background is `#fefefe`, the decoded colour of the illustration's flat sky, so the sky added above the image when stacked copy is taller than `1858:941` continues it without a seam. A conditional mask fades the top of the image only by that overflow (at most 14% on tablet, 18% on phones; 0px at 1440px+), so the branches in the top right corner never show a cut edge.
- Free sky in the artwork (`1858×941`, % of its width and height): `x 0-44%` at `65%`, `46-52%` at `62%`, `54-57%` at `58%`, `59-64%` at `45%`, `66-80%` from `39%` to `28%` (the eaves), `x 82%` and beyond from the top edge (the branches). Horizontal limits keep desktop columns within `x <= 59.8%`, legal lines `<= 52%`, and the signature `<= 45%`.
- Phones (under 640px): brand (logo, tagline, icons in a row), Services and Company in two columns, Contact, then the centred legal row and signature. The image is `max(50.646cqw, 290px)` tall, cropped with `object-position: 88% 100%` so both teammates show whole, shoes included; the copy ends where the image begins.
- 640-919px: brand on top, the three columns in a row, the full 100% width `1858×941` illustration below with zero left fade. The legal lines and signature sit on the left within 50% of the width.
- 920-1199px: brand in one row (logo, tagline, icons), the three columns below it within 60.5% of the width, legal lines within 50% and the signature within 45%.
- 1200px and wider (Metricra layout): a narrow 84px brand column with the logo and the two social icons stacked vertically below it, then Services, Company and Contact on the sky, ending at 59.8% of the width. The tagline stays in `.ect-footer__brand` in the DOM (`display: contents`) and shows under the columns; the legal lines and signature follow on the left.
- Column labels are 17px bold paragraphs on phones, 15-16px semibold on desktop. Links are 15px on phones, 14px from 920px and 15px from 1440px, with 44px touch targets on touch screens (including the signature pill) and 28px rows on desktop with a fine pointer.
- The WebP comes in `768×389`, `1152×583`, `1536×778` and `1858×941` (native, so wide screens never upscale) under `wp-content/ect-pages/footer/balconul-tocilarilor-*.webp`; phones request the 580px candidate because the image is cropped wider than the screen.
- The footer runs edge to edge with no gutter, border or rounded corners; the bottom wave of the contact form meets its near-white sky.
- The signature pill is `#f1f1f3` without a border on the sky (44px min-height on phones), turning `#fd8649` with `#ffffff` text on hover/focus.
- `button.ect-consent-open` ("Setări cookie-uri") is hidden until `consent.js` runs and then reopens the `CookieConsent` banner.
