# Footer

The shared footer is a full-width band with an illustration across its whole surface: two teammates on an office balcony, one on an orange beanbag with a laptop, the other in a VR headset, above a tree-lined city. The links sit on the open white sky at the top left, as in the Metricra reference; the scene fills the bottom and climbs up the right side under the orange eaves and a tree canopy.

`footer.ect-footer` retains its Elementor classes for the fixed back-to-top control. The `.ect-footer__top` grid contains the brand, two navigation landmarks and the contact column with the address and hours. Legal links and the coffee-cup signature follow inside `.ect-footer__inner`. The decorative `.ect-footer__art` layer is hidden from assistive technology. The DOM order is the same at every width.

- The footer background is `#fefefe`, the decoded colour of the illustration's flat sky, so the sky added above the image when the copy is taller than 16:9 continues it without a seam. A mask fades the top of the image only by the height of that added sky (at most 18%, 22% on phones), so the branches in the top right corner never show a cut edge.
- Free sky in the artwork (first non-sky pixel, % of its width and height): x 0-38% at 65%, 40-46% at 62%, 48-52% at 58%, 54-60% at 45%, 62-78% from 39% to 28% (the eaves), x 80% and beyond from the top edge (the branches). Horizontal limits are set in % of the footer width so the copy keeps its place as the image scales; `padding-bottom` on `.ect-footer__inner` keeps the lower part of the image clear.
- Phones (under 640px): brand (logo, tagline, icons in a row), Services and Company in two columns, Contact, then the centred legal row and signature. The image is `max(56.25cqw, 300px)` tall, cropped with `object-position: 85% 100%` so both teammates show whole, shoes included; the copy ends where the image begins.
- 640-919px: brand on top, the three columns in a row, the image full width at 16:9. The legal lines and signature sit on the left within 50% of the width; the copy ends at 46% of the image height.
- 920-1199px: brand in one row (logo, tagline, icons), the three columns below it within 60% of the width, legal lines within 50% and the signature within 45%; the copy ends at 58% of the image height.
- 1200px and wider (Metricra layout): a narrow brand column with the 88px logo and the two social icons stacked vertically below it, then Services, Company and Contact on the sky, ending before 60% of the width. The tagline stays in `.ect-footer__brand` in the DOM (`display: contents`) and shows under the columns; the legal lines and signature follow on the left.
- Column labels are 17px bold paragraphs on phones, 15-16px semibold on desktop. Links are 15px on phones, 14px from 920px and 15px from 1440px, with 44px touch targets; the rows are 32px on desktop with a fine pointer.
- The WebP comes in 768×432, 1152×648, 1536×864 and 1672×941 (native, so wide screens never upscale) under `wp-content/ect-pages/footer/balconul-tocilarilor-*.webp`; phones request the 540px candidate because the image is cropped wider than the screen.
- The footer runs edge to edge with no gutter, border or rounded corners; the bottom wave of the contact form meets its near-white sky.
- The signature pill is `#f1f1f3` without a border on the sky, orange (`#fd8649`) with white text on hover/focus.
- `button.ect-consent-open` ("Setări cookie-uri") is hidden until `consent.js` runs and then reopens the `CookieConsent` banner.
