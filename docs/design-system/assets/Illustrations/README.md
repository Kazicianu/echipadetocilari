# Illustrations

Two kinds of pictures carry the "tocilari" idea: flat cartoon nerds (animated, on the homepage) and warm 3D characters (the service pages and the FAQ).

Cartoon nerds, Lottie animations on the homepage (Elementor Lottie widget, looping):

- `mascot-propeller-hat.lottie.json`: the homepage hero (512 × 512, 90 frames at 30 fps). A bearded nerd whose propeller hat lifts off in a puff of bubbles. `mascot-propeller-hat.png` is a still at three quarters of the loop.
- `mascot-meditating.lottie.json`: beside "ECHIPA DE TOCILARI" (520 × 520, about 121 frames at 29.97 fps). A nerd with glasses and a tie, floating cross-legged. `mascot-meditating.png` is a still at mid-loop.

3D characters:

- `faq-client-3d.webp` (640 × 640): the bearded client in a black apron with a parcel and a question-mark bubble, beside the FAQ on the homepage and the store page (280px on desktop, 160 to 180px on phones). Alt text on the site: "Client 3D cu barbă și șorț negru, care ține un colet sub braț și ridică degetul lângă o bulă cu semnul întrebării".
- `faq-tocilar-3d-v2.webp` (640 × 640): the nerd with round glasses and a laptop, standing on the top edge of the store's orange "Pentru cine e" panel with `shadow-character` (150 to 240px wide).
- `vr.webp` (1018 × 1200): a bearded man in a VR headset jumping, the /creare-site-web/ hero.
- `Tocilar-creare-site-web.webp` (1000 × 750): the "Design și UX" section of /creare-site-web/ (alt "Tocilar care ofera servicii de creare site web" on the site; write it with diacritics in new work).
- `work.png` (1260 × 870): a developer at a desk, the picture tile of the website-types bento on /creare-site-web/.
- `character-4FKAQPE.png` (935 × 380): a website mock-up with tiny figures, plants and charts, beside "Ce îți oferă un site de prezentare" on /creare-site-web/.
- `click.png` (225 × 225): a 3D orange mouse pointer, decoration in the "Design și UX" section.

Store page drawings (inline SVG on the site, several colours each; copied as files):

- `box-buddy.svg`: a cardboard box with a face and an `orange` tape, hopping beside the metro section title.
- `delivery-van.svg`: the small `orange` van that drives along the metro line.

Put illustrations on `white` or on the blob backgrounds; reserve their space with `width` and `height`. Give `alt` text when the picture adds something the text does not say, and leave it empty when it repeats a heading.
