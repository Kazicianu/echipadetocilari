# Illustrations

Two kinds of pictures carry the "tocilari" idea: flat cartoon nerds (animated) and 3D characters at work.

Cartoon nerds, Lottie animations from the homepage (Elementor Lottie widget, looping):

- `mascot-propeller-hat.lottie.json`: the hero of the homepage (originally `dlf10_Tu6NPeCh0d.json`, 512 × 512, 90 frames at 30 fps). A bearded nerd whose propeller hat lifts off in a puff of bubbles. `mascot-propeller-hat.png` is a still at three quarters of the loop.
- `mascot-meditating.lottie.json`: the "Echipa de Tocilari" section (originally `dlf10_7fCbvNSmFD.json`, 520 × 520, about 121 frames at 29.97 fps). A nerd with glasses and a tie, floating cross-legged. `mascot-meditating.png` is a still at mid-loop.

3D characters, used as hero pictures on the service pages:

- `Tocilar-creare-site-web.webp`: website design page (1000 × 750).
- `administrare-site-echipa-de-tocilari.webp`: website maintenance page (1334 × 746).
- `echipa-de-tocilari-servicii-seo.webp`: SEO page (907 × 713).
- `work.png`: Google Ads hero (1260 × 870; the page serves the 768 and 1024px versions with `srcset`).

Put illustrations on `white` or on the blob backgrounds. Reserve their space with `width` and `height` attributes so the layout does not jump. The service pages give them a descriptive `alt` ("Tocilar care oferă servicii de creare site web"); the Google Ads hero leaves it empty because the heading says the same thing.
